PedalFour

Four-bar pedal desk. Suno and loop DAWs move the root with every chord so nothing ever leans. Session players plant a pedal — bass, pad, or both — under four bars of changing harmony so tension builds, then the next section resolves. Live piano, upright, nylon, kit, trumpet, violin. Not BreakFour (air before last chorus). Not LiftTwo (climb into the hook). Not TurnFour (section hand-off). Not PreEight or AfterHook.
