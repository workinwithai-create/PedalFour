# PedalFour

Four-bar live pedal-point desk for bedroom producers and AI-music finishers.

Suno and loop-based DAWs move the root with every chord so the track never leans. Session players plant a pedal — bass, pad, or both — under four bars of changing harmony so tension builds, then the next section resolves. PedalFour seats live chairs on that cut, plays pocket vs pedal, and exports a punch list.

Live samples only — FluidR3 acoustic grand, upright bass, nylon guitar, trumpet (lead stand-in), violin, and an acoustic kit from the PreEight sample CDN. No oscillators. Audio never leaves the tab.

## Loop

1. Pick a groove (Amber Walk, Porch Climb, Fold Radio, Stair House, Carbon Verse).
2. Hear A: the pocket. Four bars of normal root motion. It loops. Nothing leans.
3. Stamp a pedal recipe.
4. Hear B: four of pocket, four of pedal. Then it stops. The next section would resolve on 9.
5. Copy the punch list. Drop the idea on the four bars before the section that needs to land.

Space plays B. A is pocket. 8 is pedal only.
